Research agenda

Spatial evidence for healthier, safer, and more equitable communities

My research examines the spatial and social determinants of violence, public safety, and population health. I integrate theory-driven inquiry with GIS, spatial data science, AI-informed analysis, and community partnership.

01

Violence, Public Safety & Population Health

Street and urban violence, gender-based violence, sexual exploitation, crisis response, and maternal and mental health.

02

GIS, Spatial Data Science & AI

Spatial modeling, human mobility, machine learning, large language models, and data visualization.

03

Place, Social Environment & Inequality

Built environments, social determinants, racial and ethnic disparities, equity, and community vulnerability.

Selected work

Projects

Current focus

Violence, Crisis Response & Population Health

Using 911, 988, 211, and Violence Experiences Survey data to examine patterns of gender-based violence, mental health needs, sexual exploitation, social vulnerability, and access to services across Louisiana and the Gulf South.

Selected research products:

Maternity Care Report: A 2026 Report on Louisiana (Newcomb Institute, 2026) Louisiana's Demographic and Health Landscape: Trends, Inequities, and Policy Pathways (Newcomb Institute, 2026)

Climate, Disasters & Human Mobility

Investigating how climate change, extreme weather, and natural disasters reshape human mobility and spatial accessibility for diverse social groups.

Selected research product:

Exploring Dynamic Human Mobility of Diverse Social Groups Under Extreme Heat Conditions: A Simulation-Based Approach (I-GUIDE, 2025)

Population Change, Mobility & Crime

Examining how mobility shifts, facility closures, population composition, and racial heterogeneity shape urban crime patterns and equity, including changes during COVID-19 lockdowns.

Selected research products:

Measuring perceived racial heterogeneity and its impact on crime: An ambient population-based approach (Cities, 2023) Examining the Impact of Mobility Reduction and Facility Closure during COVID-19 on Crime (OhioLINK Electronic Theses and Dissertations Center, 2024) The Impact of Changing Population Composition and Diversity on Crime during COVID-19 Lockdowns (Applied Geography, 2025)

Built Environment, Infrastructure & Street-Level Crime

Studying how streets, transportation infrastructure, points of interest, and perceived environmental conditions influence crime risks and patterns at the street and neighborhood levels.

Selected publications:

Using Google Street View imagery to capture micro built environment characteristics in drug places, compared with street robbery (CEUS, 2021) A Multiscale Assessment of the Impact of Perceived Safety from Street View Imagery on Street Crime (Annals of AAG, 2024) Capturing the Spatial Arrangement of POIs in Crime Modeling (CEUS, 2025) From Alleys to Arterial Streets: Unpacking the Influence of Diverse Street Types and Associated Business on Urban Theft Patterns (Applied Geography, 2025) Assessing the impact of modern streetcar on street robberies: a longitudinal comparison in Cincinnati, OH (Habitat International, 2025)
