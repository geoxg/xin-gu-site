GIScience · Spatial Data Science · AI for Public Good

Xin Gu, PhD

Postdoctoral Fellow, Newcomb Institute at Tulane University

I study how place, mobility, climate, the built and social environment, and inequality shape violence, public safety, and population health.

My research combines GIS, spatial modeling, data visualization, and AI-informed methods with community-engaged, policy-relevant scholarship. Current projects examine gender-based violence, sexual exploitation, crisis-service utilization, maternal and mental health, and community vulnerability across Louisiana and the Gulf South.

I earned my PhD in Geographic Information Science from the University of Cincinnati and master's degrees in Geospatial Information Science and Criminal Justice from the University of Maryland, College Park.

Interdisciplinary and publicly engaged
