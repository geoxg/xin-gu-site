Welcome to my homepage!

I’m currently a Postdoctoral fellow at Tulane University. My research uses geospatial data and AI-informed tools to understand how place, mobility, climate, physical environments, and social structures shape violence, public health, and social outcomes.

I completed my Ph.D. in Geographic Information Science at the University of Cincinnati, where I investigated the impact of COVID-19 mobility reductions and facility closures on crime. I also hold master’s degrees in Geospatial Information Science and Criminal Justice from the University of Maryland College Park.

I’ve led and contributed to multiple research, and have published in top journals like Applied Geography, Annals of AAG, CEUS, Habitat International, and Cities. I'm passionate about blending spatial computing and AI technology, quantitative and qualitative data analytics and visualization to solve real-world challenges and serve for public good.
